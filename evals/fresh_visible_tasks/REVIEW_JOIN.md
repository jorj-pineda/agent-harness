# Pure selected-byte fresh review join

`review_fresh_run` accepts bounded report, copied-regrade and review bytes with
independently selected hashes. It performs no filesystem access, provider/backend
allocation, grading or admission. The existing pure fresh summary checks the fixed
twelve-cell task/arm/repeat schedule and raw result/preservation bindings.

The join binds manifest/report/regrade/review hashes, all twelve review and copied
grade identities, artifact pins, original outcomes and three task-specific original
criterion hashes. A criterion for one task cannot substitute for another. Copied
grade agreement flags must match their recorded outcomes. Missing execution cannot
inherit a clean review, regrade or source-preservation claim. Reviewer names and
external evidence references remain unauthenticated caller assertions; references
are not opened or verified by the join.

Output keeps all six paired original-criteria acceptance comparisons, task/arm
counts, disagreement ordinals and unknown review coverage. Completion, verification,
visible checks, artifact acceptance and settlement stay separate. A started result
or unknown usage cannot become complete coverage. Missing records remain unknown.

The frozen promising-follow-up rule is only eligible for human review when a
complete known live-labeled cohort has more stronger completed-and-accepted outcomes
in aggregate, no task-level loss, no paired copied-acceptance regression, matching
original/copied grades and complete clean coverage without rescue, weakening, misuse
or other regression. Synthetic live-label fixtures can test that logic but do not
constitute live evidence. Scripted reports are always ineligible. Benefit stays
`not_assessed`; no reviewer authentication, actual approval, default or release
conclusion follows. This batch remains offline; the frozen candidate is not admitted.
