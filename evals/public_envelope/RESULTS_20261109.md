## Next decision

explicit bool requirement remains a demonstrated failure. Preserve this cohort
unchanged. A follow-up should investigate that exact requirement/check gap before
adding tools, changing defaults or claiming a reliable feature envelope. Fresh
held-out, intended-model and real-project workflow evidence remains required for
release.
