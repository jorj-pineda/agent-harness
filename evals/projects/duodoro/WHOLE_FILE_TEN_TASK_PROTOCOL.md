## Question and reason for this slice

Does the existing whole-file preset produce acceptable, completed, checked changes
across the ten agreed tasks within their original limits, rather than only on the
formatter? The selected [preset observations](TOOLSET_RESULTS.md) accepted and
completed two whole-file formatter attempts. Later CLI/panel recovery variants
also passed their exercised sequences, but involved an already correct formatter
patch. They provide no evidence of generalization to the other tasks or G3's
eight-of-ten target.

This follow-up changes no application feature, default, prompt retention strategy,
acceptance contract or budget. It applies an existing preset to the original broad
candidate envelope before claiming usefulness. Whole-file writes have no exact-edit
stale-hash protection and may overwrite unrelated content or consume substantial
output on larger files. Preserve that risk and grade every final artifact.
